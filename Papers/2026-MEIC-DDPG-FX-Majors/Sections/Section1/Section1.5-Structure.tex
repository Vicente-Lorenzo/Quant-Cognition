\subsection{Structure}

The rest of this document is organised as follows. Section~\ref{Section:Background} covers the material needed to follow the text: how the foreign exchange market works, how technical analysis summarises a price series, and how neural networks and reinforcement learning combine into the methods used here, ending with DDPG. Section~\ref{Section:RelatedWork} reviews the published applications of these methods to trading, grouped by market and by algorithm, and states what the existing work leaves open. Section~\ref{Section:Implementation} describes the system that was built: the framework and its connection to cTrader, the backtesting engine and its cost model, and the design of the observation, the action and the reward. Section~\ref{Section:ExperimentalResults} reports the evaluation protocol, the result for each of the seven pairs, how the agents behave while trading, what the trading cost, the robustness of the results to the entry path, the attribution of the returns into policy and market exposure, the screening that rejected weaker candidates, what happened in the year held back from training, and the outcome of running all seven together as a portfolio. Section~\ref{Section:Conclusion} summarises what was achieved, states the limitations of the study and describes the work that follows from it.
