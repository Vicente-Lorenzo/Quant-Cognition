\section{Introduction}
\label{Section:Introduction}

The foreign exchange market is the largest financial market in the world, with an average turnover of 9.6 trillion US dollars per day in April 2025 \cite{bis_otc_2025}, and it trades continuously from Sunday evening to Friday evening. This makes it a good target for automated trading. A program can act at any hour, the major currency pairs absorb retail order sizes without moving the price, and the price history is available down to individual quotes. The signal, however, is weak. Returns are close to unpredictable over short horizons \cite{fama_efficient_1970}, and the cost of trading is high enough that many strategies which look profitable in a simplified test lose money once those costs are charged.

This work applies Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) to that problem. One Deep Deterministic Policy Gradient (DDPG) \cite{lillicrap_continuous_2015} agent is trained for each of the seven major currency pairs and evaluated over eleven years of tick data, charging the spread quoted at the time of the trade and the commission of a retail account.
